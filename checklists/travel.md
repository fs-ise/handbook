# Travel checklist

## Planning and approval

- [ ] Define trip purpose, destination, and dates
- [ ] Identify relevant deadlines and planned activities
- [ ] Check the [policy for travel expenses, business entertainment, gifts, and other expenses](https://nc-2272638881871040784.nextcloud-ionos.com/index.php/apps/files/files/2052508?dir=/management/compliance&openfile=true) / [German version](https://nc-2272638881871040784.nextcloud-ionos.com/index.php/apps/files/files/2052509?dir=%2Fmanagement%2Fcompliance&openfile=true)
- [ ] Submit the [business travel request (Dienstreiseantrag)](https://forms.office.com/pages/responsepage.aspx?id=avrDopl3l0WOtmeG27ygke2r1eR1BLpIvnZ_q46oMYxUMFVEVTM2TENQNThZNUtZSldPRENOM0VDTC4u&route=shorturl)
- [ ] Obtain travel approval before booking
- [ ] Estimate travel costs and identify funding sources
- [ ] Select the budget that will fund the trip:

  | Budget | Approver | Cost center | Cost object | Project number |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Research budget (personal budget) | Ansgar Richter | 1700 | 175999 | 180049 |
  | Research funding | Frank Ecker | 1905 | 19999 | 209999 |

- [ ] Apply for external funding, if applicable
- [ ] Request advance payment, if needed
- [ ] Check flight and accommodation booking deadlines, including conference acceptance and registration deadlines

## Bookings and registration

- [ ] Register for conference/event
- [ ] Check membership requirements and discounts
- [ ] Book transportation (flights, trains, etc.) after approval, privately or through [TravelPerk](https://app.travelperk.com/login)
- [ ] If booking privately, ensure the hotel invoice is addressed to Frankfurt School and includes its address
- [ ] If booking through TravelPerk, select the budget that will fund the trip; Frankfurt School's address is added automatically and costs are paid directly
- [ ] Book accommodation
- [ ] Arrange airport and local transfers
- [ ] Check passport, visa, and entry requirements for every destination and transit country, including connecting flights; verify that any required travel authorizations are valid
- [ ] Check travel insurance coverage
- [ ] Save booking confirmations and invoices in the trip folder
- [ ] Download offline maps and save the hotel, venue, and transfer points

## Preparation

- [ ] Share travel dates with colleagues, HIWIs, and relevant seminar or thesis students; coordinate work and coverage
- [ ] Arrange teaching, meetings, and supervision coverage
- [ ] Schedule meetings with collaborators
- [ ] Prepare presentation and other materials
- [ ] Check mobile connectivity and roaming charges; consider an eSIM or local connectivity option where useful
- [ ] Check that payment cards will work abroad, identify a backup payment method, and check currency and cash requirements
- [ ] Save your bank's contact details and card-blocking instructions offline, separately from your payment cards
- [ ] Prepare equipment, adapters, and clothing
- [ ] Back up important files on devices you plan to bring and make sure you can access the backups
- [ ] Install important device updates and charge devices and power banks
- [ ] Download the itinerary, booking details, addresses, and key contact numbers for offline access; keep paper copies if useful
- [ ] Bring health-insurance details and any required vaccination or medication documents for the destination
- [ ] Identify a reliable local public-transport app or other mobility options
- [ ] Review itinerary and set travel reminders

## During the trip

- [ ] Keep receipts and proof of payment
- [ ] Retain boarding passes and transport tickets
- [ ] Record expenses not covered by invoices
- [ ] Request missing invoices or payment receipts from airlines or booking providers
- [ ] Document relevant meetings and follow-up actions

## Reimbursement

- [ ] Collect and organize all receipts
- [ ] Prepare expense overview
- [ ] Submit travel expense claim (Reisekostenabrechnung)
- [ ] Submit external funding reimbursement claims, if applicable
- [ ] Reconcile advance payments and third-party funding
- [ ] Verify reimbursement payments
- [ ] Follow up on outstanding reimbursements
- [ ] Set a reminder to check the status of the reimbursement claim

## Follow-up and archiving

- [ ] Send follow-up messages to collaborators
- [ ] Share relevant research materials and outcomes
- [ ] Complete outstanding trip-related deliverables
- [ ] Document lessons learned
- [ ] Archive travel documents in the trip folder
- [ ] Close the trip
